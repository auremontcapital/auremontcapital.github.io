# Auremont Capital — bilingual corporate site

A responsive, independent corporate website template inspired by the clean editorial language of European venture-capital websites. It is **not** an official Rocket Internet website or an exact reproduction.

## Quick start
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation, dependency download, build step or server is required for the basic website. Use **PT / EN** in the header to switch Portuguese (Portugal) and English. Your preference is saved locally by the browser.

## Pages
- Home: `#/`
- Approach: `#/approach`
- Portfolio: `#/portfolio`
- About: `#/about`
- Insights: `#/insights`
- Contact: `#/contact`
- Legal: `#/legal`

## Where to edit
- `brand-config.js`: brand palette, contact details, legal identifiers, real investment holdings, news and team information.
- `script.js`: all European Portuguese and English copy is in `strings.pt` and `strings.en`. Keep both languages in sync.
- `styles.css`: layout and responsive styling.
- `favicon.svg`: original Auremont monogram, editable SVG.

## Before publishing
1. Verify legal right to use the brand name, domain and logo; perform Portuguese/EU company name and trademark checks.
2. Replace the sample Lisbon location and `contacto@exemplo.pt` with real, confirmed details. The contact form intentionally refuses to send to the placeholder address.
3. Add the proper legal entity, company number, address and disclosures required in your jurisdiction. Confirm any applicable financial-service regulatory obligations with legal counsel.
4. Add **only verified** portfolio investments, news and actual people; never present demo content as track record or licensed status.
5. Prepare an appropriate privacy notice if analytics, a contact backend, maps or other third-party tools are added.
6. Test with a hosted preview / production server on mobile and desktop.

This is a front-end-only static website. The contact form uses `mailto:` after a real email is configured; no backend, CMS, database or newsletter service is included.
